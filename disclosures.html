5. FINRA Intraday Margin and Leverage Disclosure

FINRA has replaced the prior day-trading margin requirements with new intraday margin standards. Under the new framework, the prior $25,000 pattern day trader minimum equity requirement and trade-count-based pattern day trader designation have been eliminated. However, margin trading still requires minimum account equity, broker approval, and ongoing compliance with the broker's margin requirements.

Eligible margin accounts generally remain subject to a minimum equity requirement of at least $2,000, and brokerage firms may impose higher requirements, additional restrictions, real-time risk controls, liquidation procedures, or other account-specific limitations. Intraday buying power, margin availability, and trading permissions may vary by broker, account type, security, strategy, volatility, and market conditions.

Trading on margin, using leverage, or engaging in frequent intraday trading significantly increases risk and may result in rapid losses, margin calls, forced liquidations, account restrictions, or losses greater than the amount deposited. You are solely responsible for understanding and complying with your broker's current margin requirements, risk controls, and account restrictions before placing any trade.
