IMPORTANT CONTEXT

What Performance Numbers Cannot Tell You.

Performance numbers can help show:

  • Trade frequency during a period
  • Win/loss count
  • Total reported P&L
  • Average contracts and days held
  • Strategy type and documentation

Performance numbers cannot guarantee:

  • Future returns or similar results
  • Every member will trade the same way
  • Every setup will be profitable
  • Personalized suitability or risk alignment

Performance is one piece of the picture. Risk management, position sizing, timing, execution, and personal decision-making all matter.
